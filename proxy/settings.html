Values are the controller's own reports. The settings are not part of the normal report, so in hybrid and local mode the server adds them to the read list on start, every SETTINGS_READ_MINUTES and when you press "Read settings now", the same way the cloud's service panel does. A write is sent on every poll until the controller reports the new value. Ranges are the ones the cloud panels offer, narrowed by the minimum and maximum the controller reports.
