Every register the controller reported (or the server asked for), with the latest value. Index 1 is the value, 3 and 4 its minimum and maximum. A value only counts once two reports in a row agree; a newer raw value is marked as unconfirmed until the next report, and as a possible glitch only if it looks corrupted (a huge jump or outside the setting range). New registers, for example ones the cloud's service panel reads, are added automatically. Refreshes every 10 s.
